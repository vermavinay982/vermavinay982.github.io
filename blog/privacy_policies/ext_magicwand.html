Privacy Policy for Magic Cursor Wand

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Magic Cursor Wand" Chrome Extension handles your data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Magic Cursor Wand is designed to run entirely locally within your web browser.

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, or tracking data.
  • The extension only reads your mouse movement coordinates on the specific page you are actively viewing in order to render the graphical canvas overlays (the trails and glitter). This cursor data is processed in real-time and is never recorded or saved.

2. Local Storage

The extension uses your browser's built-in storage (chrome.storage.sync) strictly to save your visual preferences (e.g., your selected cursor color, trail size, and whether Chalk Mode is enabled). This data remains tied to your local browser profile and is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

3. Third-Party Services and Tracking

Magic Cursor Wand does not contain any analytics tracking, advertisements, or third-party background scripts. It operates entirely independently on your device and does not communicate with external servers while you browse.

4. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change in future updates, we will update this Privacy Policy and reflect the date of the update at the top of this document.

5. Contact

For any questions, bug reports, or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the support tab on the Chrome Web Store listing.
